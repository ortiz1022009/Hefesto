If Android says the developer isn't verified

From late 2026, in some countries Android blocks apps from unregistered developers. Hefesto still installs through Android's advanced flow, meant for people who know what they're installing:

  1. Turn on developer options: Settings › About phone › tap “Build number” seven times.
  2. Settings › System › Developer options › “Apps from unverified developers” › turn on “Allow”. Confirm with your screen lock.
  3. Android asks whether someone is guiding or pressuring you. Confirm only if the decision is yours.
  4. Restart the phone. A 24-hour safety wait begins.
  5. After 24 hours, go back to that same screen and choose “7 days” or “Indefinitely”. To receive updates without repeating the process, choose indefinitely.
  6. Open the APK. You will see one last warning; tap “Install anyway”.

If you have a computer at hand, installing with ADB needs none of this. The same text is in the app: Settings › Help › Install and update.

Check it's the official APK.
