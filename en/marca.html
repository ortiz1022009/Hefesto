Description

Short · for linksHefesto is a free Android app for creating websites, apps, games and scripts from your phone, with an AI agent and a full Linux inside.
Long · for articlesHefesto turns an Android phone into a software workshop. It brings a full Linux without root, compilers (Python, Node, Go, Rust, Java, Android SDK, Godot 4) and an agent that plans, writes, runs and fixes in your own words. It has no account, ads or servers of its own: you bring the intelligence with your key or a local model. Created by Diego Ortiz, it's distributed as a signed APK from GitHub.
